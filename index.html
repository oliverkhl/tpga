About Two Parrots Sourcing & Origin

Two Parrots Trading Solutions is a social agribusiness committed to building sustainable and inclusive value chains in Cambodia. We connect farmers, producers, and markets through innovative production, processing, sourcing, and trading solutions that ensure quality and traceability from farm to market.

With 20 years of agri-food experience, Two Parrots bridges smallholder farmers, local partners, and international buyers through a transparent, fair-trade, and sustainability-driven approach that creates shared value for all. We work across the value chain—from farm-level sourcing and processing coordination to quality control and shipment readiness—so buyers can purchase with confidence.

From Origin to Shipment: How We Work

We organize supply with clear specifications, lot-level traceability, and practical quality checks aligned with buyer requirements. Our team supports sampling, documentation, packing formats, and shipment coordination. The result is an export-ready supply process designed to reduce risk, improve consistency, and strengthen long-term sourcing programs.
