Our Vision

Building Long-Term Agricultural Supply Infrastructure Between Origin & Australia

01

Direct Origin Traceability

Establishing verifiable farm-to-container supply chains across Cambodia and Southeast Asia for Geographical Indication (GI) Kampot pepper and milled agricultural starches.

02

Strict Biosecurity Governance

Enforcing steam sterilization, ≤12% moisture thresholds, 0.00% foreign matter standards, and full DAFF/BICON documentation prior to Australian port arrival.

03

Commercial Market Execution

Empowering subsidiary KH Trading Group to serve Australian supermarkets, food processors, and foodservice distributors with reliable carton and pallet fulfilment.
