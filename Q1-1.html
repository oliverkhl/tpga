3. Key Compliance & Financial Considerations

Customs Tariffs & FTAs: Goods originating in Cambodia qualify for duty-free entry into Australia under AANZFTA or RCEP, provided a valid Certificate of Origin (COO) is supplied.
Import GST Deferred Scheme (DGST): Import GST (10%) is calculated on Customs Value + Duty + Transport/Insurance. Registering for Deferred GST defers border payment to monthly BAS returns.
Superannuation Compliance: Statutory Employer Superannuation Guarantee (11.5%) must be remitted quarterly to compliant Australian super funds before statutory due dates.
