Pre-Order & Pre-Shipment Product Compliance Action Checklist

Mandatory verification steps before placing orders or loading containers from Cambodia to Australia

DAFF & FSANZ Compliant

PHASE 1: PRE-ORDER CHECKLIST (Before Placing Order)

  • DAFF Import Permit for Seasoning Powder (No. 20) Apply for Australian DAFF Biosecurity permit for dehydrated chicken (7.19%) BEFORE placing PO to avoid border destruction.
  • BICON Biosecurity Query (Whole Pepper & Rice) Verify BICON conditions for Whole Pepper (No. 8-9) devitalisation and Milled Rice (No. 19) stored pest controls.
  • FSANZ Food Additive & Sweetener Audit Check MSG (INS 621), Ribonucleotides (INS 635) & Sucralose limits in Instant Noodles (No. 14) against FSANZ Code 1.3.1.
  • PEAL Plain English Allergen Artwork Review Ensure packaging artwork explicitly bolds allergens (Wheat, Sesame, Soy, Coconut) and contains summary box.

PHASE 2: PRE-SHIPMENT CHECKLIST (Before Loading Container)

  • Phytosanitary Certificate from Cambodian Gov Obtain official Phytosanitary Cert for Whole Pepper (No. 8-9) and Milled Rice (No. 19) confirming free from insects/seeds.
  • Microbiological Certificate of Analysis (COA) Require batch test COAs showing negative Salmonella and Aflatoxin compliance for Peppercorns and Flours.
  • Heat-Treatment & Flowchart Statement Obtain official processing flowchart and thermal declaration for Seasoning Powder (No. 20) and Noodles (No. 14).
  • ISPM-15 Wood Packing & Container Declaration Sign Packing Declaration stating container cleanliness and ISPM-15 treatment for timber pallets used.
